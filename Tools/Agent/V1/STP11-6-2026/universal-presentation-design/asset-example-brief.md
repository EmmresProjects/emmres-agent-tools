# Neighborhood Water Resilience
Audience: City resilience committee
Outcome: Approve a phased pilot and budget release

## Context
Three districts report repeated street flooding. Drainage upgrades are currently planned independently.

## Evidence
- Reported incidents rose from 18 in Q1 to 31 in Q3.
- A shared pilot can coordinate design and measurement.
- Example data must be replaced with verified municipal records.

## Decision
Approve phase-one funding, name an accountable owner, and confirm the measurement plan.
