# Q4 board review — brief

Goal: Ask the board to endorse the FY27 plan and a 22% reallocation of
acquisition spend toward expansion and partner enablement.

Audience: Nine board members, including two investor directors; half read the
pre-read on mobile, so the first three slides must stand alone.

Constraints:
- 25 minute slot with 15 minutes of discussion.
- Use the corporate blue brand preset; no new photography.
- All revenue figures are unaudited management reporting and must carry that caption.
- No more than 12 slides in the main deck.

Success criteria:
- The board can restate the recommendation in one sentence after the meeting.
- Every revenue claim traces to a chart or table on the same slide.
- The deck passes the accessibility gate with zero errors.

Deliverable: editable .pptx plus a preview PDF for the pre-read email.

Brand: corporate-blue

Length: 12 slides / 25 minutes

Avoid:
- Defending the SMB miss with market context.
- More than four KPI cards on any slide.
