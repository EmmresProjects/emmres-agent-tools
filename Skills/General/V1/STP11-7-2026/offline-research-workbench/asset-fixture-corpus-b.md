# Journal announces retractions after investigation (fixture source B)

Manila — The journal announced on 5 March 2024 that it had retracted a paper after an investigation into data fabrication. The publisher said corrections would be published with a DOI and linked to the original article.

The announcement repeated the wording of a press release issued by the publisher two days earlier. Peer review records are retained for ten years under the policy, the notice said.

A separate notice in 2023 recorded an expression of concern about image duplication in two papers. The publisher stated that both papers were retracted and that a correction notice was issued on 12 November 2023.
