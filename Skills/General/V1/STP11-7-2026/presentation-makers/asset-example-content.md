# Q4 retention working notes

Source material for the example brief. Markdown headings become content sections,
`- ` lines become slide points, `Metric: label = value` lines become metric cards,

## Situation

- Enterprise renewals are concentrated in Q4: 62% of annual contract value renews between October and December.
- Support handles renewals reactively, so the busiest support month is also the riskiest renewal month.
- The Q3 review pack committed to a retention plan before the November board meeting.
- Source: FY26 Q3 business review, slide 14

## Complication

- Silent churn in the 90-day pre-renewal window rose from 4.1% to 7.3% year over year.
- Median first response time in that window rose from 3.2 hours to 8.6 hours.
- Two of the three lost accounts in Q3 had open support tickets older than 14 days at renewal.
- The hiring freeze blocks the three support roles requested in August.
- Metric: Pre-renewal churn = 7.3% (up 3.2 pts year over year)
- Metric: Median first response = 8.6 h (target 4 h)
- Source: Support operations warehouse, extract dated 2026-10-28

## Insight

- Accounts that received a scheduled 30-minute pre-renewal check-in churned at 2.4%, against 9.1% for accounts with no check-in.
- The gap is concentrated in accounts above 100k ARR, which are only 18% of the base but 54% of revenue at risk.
- Check-ins are already being delivered informally by two team leads; the work exists but is invisible and unmeasured.
- Source: Renewal cohort analysis, 412 accounts, 2025-2026

## Recommendation

- Convert the informal check-in into a standard 30-minute milestone owned by the account team.
- Reallocate 0.6 FTE from the onboarding queue, which is running 22% under plan, into the pre-renewal programme.
- Instrument the check-in in the CRM so completion is measured weekly, not remembered.
- Run the pilot on the 74 accounts above 100k ARR that renew before 31 January.
- Source: Operating plan appendix B

## Impact

- Pilot scope covers 54% of revenue at risk for 0.6 FTE, no net new headcount.
- Expected churn reduction of 3.1 points on the pilot cohort, held at the conservative end of the cohort analysis.
- Payback is reached if three of the 74 accounts renew that would otherwise churn.
- Metric: Revenue protected = 2.4M ARR (pilot cohort)
- Metric: Cost = 0.6 FTE reallocated
- Metric: Break-even = 3 renewals
- Source: Finance model v4, retention scenario B

## Risks and mitigations

- Onboarding delay: onboarding is 22% under plan, so the reallocation has slack, but the queue must be reviewed weekly.
- Check-in fatigue: cap the programme at one scheduled contact per account per quarter, and reuse the existing QBR slot where one exists.
- Measurement risk: if CRM completion is not instrumented by 30 November, the pilot cannot be evaluated and should not start.
- Owner for each risk is named in the appendix.
- Source: Risk register, programme office

## Next steps

- Decision today: approve the pilot and the 0.6 FTE reallocation.
- Owner: VP Customer Success, with Support Operations delivering the weekly completion report.
- First checkpoint: 2026-12-05, pilot completion and early renewal signal.
- If the checkpoint misses, the programme returns to the committee with a stop recommendation.
- Source: Steering committee terms of reference
