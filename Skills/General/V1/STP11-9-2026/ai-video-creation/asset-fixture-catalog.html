Coastal Video Capability List

Numbered Skill Tool list   for ingest tests

Category 1: Captioning, Subtitles, and Accessibility

Category purpose: Making video understandable without sound or with different languages.

  1. 1. Skill Name: Subtitle Timing Aligner

    Description: Aligns caption cue boundaries to shot edits and validates reading speed.

  2. 2. Skill Name: Accessibility Caption Reviewer

    Description: Checks caption line length, contrast guidance, and audio-description needs.

Category 2: Safety, Rights, Consent, and Provenance

Category purpose: Protecting people and audiences across creation and distribution.

3. Skill Name: Rights and Release Tracker
Description: Records rights, consent, and license metadata for every reference asset.
4. Skill Name: Human Approval Workflow
Description: Requires a recorded human decision before sensitive or irreversible actions.
