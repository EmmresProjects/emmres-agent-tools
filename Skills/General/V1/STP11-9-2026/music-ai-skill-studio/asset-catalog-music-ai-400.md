# Music + AI Skill Catalog

Seed catalog asset for music-ai-skill-studio: 400 skill concepts across 40 categories,
covering music creation, production, research, delivery, and responsible AI workflows.

This file is the default `--source` for the studio, and it doubles as a format example.
The parser also accepts numbered lists from a wiki, copied HTML, Markdown headings, and
plain text, with or without the `Skill Name:` / `Description:` keywords.

Published scope note for every skill: it defines required inputs and constraints, produces
editable and traceable outputs, supports human revision, and validates musical intent,
technical quality, rights, accessibility, and delivery requirements.
